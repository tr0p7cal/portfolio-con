QUANTUM SENSORS

Revolutionary quantum tunneling sensors provide unprecedented sensitivity and precision. Unlike traditional prosthetics, quantum sensors detect neural signals at the sub-atomic level, enabling natural, intuitive movement control. These sensors can detect magnetic fields as small as femto-tesla (10⁻¹⁵ T) – orders of magnitude more sensitive than Earth's magnetic field.
